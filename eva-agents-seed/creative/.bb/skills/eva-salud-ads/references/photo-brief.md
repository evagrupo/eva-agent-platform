# Photo brief template (Eva Salud)

Copy this into `BRIEF.md` in the project, fill the image list, then generate (natively, via a child agent, or with `scripts/generate_image.py`).

## Global style — apply to EVERY image
- Premium, real **editorial stock photograph**, not AI: full-frame camera (Sony A7R V), 35 or 50mm lens, f/1.8–2.8, natural light. True skin texture with pores and small imperfections, natural flyaway hair, correct anatomy, realistic eyes and teeth. No plastic/waxy skin, no over-smoothing, no HDR, no oversaturation.
- **Hands:** every person has exactly two hands with five natural fingers and a believable grip. No extra or ownerless hands.
- **Devices face their users.** Phones, tablets and laptops are turned toward the person using them; the camera sees the **back** of the device (or a steep side angle where no screen content is visible). Never show a screen's content to the viewer. This avoids the physically impossible "screen faces the camera while the person looks at it" error and the mangled fingers that come with it.
- People: Spanish / Mediterranean looks, varied ages, natural and relatable (not model-perfect), casual real clothing. Emotion: confianza, calidez, empoderamiento.
- Settings: contemporary Spain — bright homes, white walls, light wood, plants, terracotta, sunlight; modern clean clinics.
- Palette soft/neutral with natural touches of green (#00983a) or navy (#232263) in clothes or props. Avoid red/orange dominance.
- **No text, logos, brand names, watermarks, or readable UI anywhere.**
- **Vertical 4:5 or taller** (largest size available, e.g. 1024×1536 high quality). Leave clean negative space where each image says.

## Per image
`<filename>.png` — scene; people (age, look, clothing); action and emotion; where the devices are and that they face the person; **negative space location** (top third / left / right).
