## 1 · Cover
`eva seguros` logo in white on brand green, with a large watermark leaf. Title: **MANUAL DE MARCA**.

## 2 · Purpose
> Este manual es la guía de aplicación de la identidad de **Grupo Eva**. Incluye la tipografía corporativa, el código cromático, las proporciones y los usos, permitidos y no permitidos, de cada una de las acepciones de la marca.

Full-bleed photo: a young woman biting an apple, daylight, natural and candid — the brand's photographic tone.

## 3–4 · 01 Nuestra historia
> **Eva Seguros.** Nace en 2021 como una compañía innovadora y disruptiva. Nuestro objetivo es democratizar servicios tan básicos como la telefonía, la energía y la salud ofreciendo el mejor servicio con los precios más competitivos del mercado.
>
> Servicios sin barreras, sin letras pequeñas ni ataduras, en EVA te quedarás por la sencillez.
>
> **MUERDE LA MANZANA.**

For ads: the promise is simplicity and no small print, and the tone is close and human — not corporate or clinical.

## 5–6 · 02 Logotipo · Composición
Three parts, always in this arrangement:
- **Hoja** (leaf) — "Representa los valores centrales de la compañía y siempre está presente."
- **eva** — "Se trata del nombre de la marca. Siempre está presente."
- **descriptor** (seguros / salud / energía / card / grupo) — "Descriptivo del sector que va junto al logotipo central a lado derecho."

So: leaf + `eva` are mandatory; only the descriptor changes per brand. Never rebuild or re-space the lockup by hand — use the supplied SVGs in `../logos/`.

## 7 · Versiones de color
Four approved treatments: **positivo** on light and on dark, **negativo** on light and on dark. In practice: green leaf + navy wordmark on light backgrounds; all-white on dark backgrounds or photos; white wordmark + green leaf on navy.

## 8 · Versiones de color de isotipo
The same four treatments for the **leaf alone**. The leaf may be used on its own as a mark or as a decorative element — always complete, never cropped or partially hidden.

## 9 · Versiones de fotografía
The logo placed over photos: a light-background version and a dark-background version. The lesson for ads: pick the logo version by the brightness of the area behind it, and if the photo is busy, give the logo a clean surface (that's the white chip used in our ad layouts).

## 10 · Aplicaciones
The family lockups shown on white and on brand green: **eva salud**, **eva energía**, **eva card**, **eva grupo**. Each sub-brand keeps the same leaf and `eva`, changing only the descriptor.

## 11–12 · 03 Colores

Each color is shown with **85 % / 70 % / 50 % / 25 %** tints, which are the approved lighter values (our mint `#e6f4eb` and lilac `#f4e8f9` come from the lowest tints).

## 13–14 · 04 Tipografía
**Noto Sans** is the corporate typeface ("tipografía principal"), shown with its full alphabet and numerals. Files in `../fonts/`.

## 15 · Tipografía / web
| Style | Weight | Size |
|---|---|---|
| Header 1 | Noto Sans **Bold** | 64 px |
| Header 2 | Noto Sans **Bold** | 48 px |
| Header 3 | Noto Sans **Bold** | 36 px |
| Subheader 1 | Regular (or bold "negrita") | 32 px |
| Subheader 2 | Regular (or bold "negrita") | 24 px |

Headings are Bold; secondary headings Regular. Ads at 1080 px wide scale these up (headline 72–104 px), keeping the same two-weight system.

## 16 · Jerarquía de usos tipográficos
- **Titular** — Noto Sans Bold
- **Subtítulo** — Noto Sans Regular
- **Cuerpo de texto** — Noto Sans Regular

Only two weights in a layout: bold for the headline, regular for everything else.

## 17–18 · 05 Fotografía
Examples: real people in everyday moments, daylight, natural expressions, phone in hand, a brand t-shirt — candid rather than posed studio work.

> **LAS FOTOS REFLEJARÁN CONFIANZA, CALIDEZ Y EMPODERAMIENTO.**

This is the standard every generated photo has to meet: trust, warmth and empowerment, in real Spanish settings, with no staged stock-photo feel.
